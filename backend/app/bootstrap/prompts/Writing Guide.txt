    ## Writing Guide Generation Methodology

    ### 1. Cognitive Baseline Analysis: Clarify the World's "Common Knowledge"
    - **Task**: Quickly distill the core "common knowledge" and "cognitive forbidden zones" from the worldview settings.
    - **Output (bullet points)**: 
        - **"Common Knowledge" List**: List no more than 3 of the most important, self-evident pieces of knowledge in this world.

    ### 2. Satisfaction Points and Pacing Planning: Design Emotional Detonation Points
    - **Task**: Plan the core satisfaction points and emotional rhythm of this volume.
    - **Output (brief description)**:

    ### 3. Conflict Distillation: Focus on the Core Opposition
    - **Task**: Distill the primary conflict of this volume.
    - **Output (one-sentence summary)**: 
        - **Core Conflict**: Use the sentence pattern "A and B come into conflict over C" to summarize this volume's core contradiction.

    ### 4. Writing Focus for This Volume: Distill the Core "Rules"
    - **Task**: Condense the above analysis into a few writing principles that **must be followed**.
    - **Output (bullet points)**: 
        - **3-5 core writing instructions**: For example: "All combat scenes in this volume must reflect the protagonist's 'plan before acting' trait", "Remember, the change in how Character A addresses the protagonist is a key marker of the progress of their relationship", "Any description involving modern objects must be approached from the native character's perspective of 'surprise' and 'bewilderment'".

    ### 5. Style and Tone

    - **Output (bullet points)**:

The instructions must be **negative constraints**, clearly stating what is **forbidden**. For example: "It is forbidden to use any superfluous metaphors that fall outside the web-novel context (such as 'like a stone thrown into a stream')", "It is forbidden to use vague words like 'as if' or 'seemingly'; replace them with direct action and psychological description", "This volume is in the buildup phase; it is forbidden to prematurely use short sentences and rhetoric that render a tense atmosphere".
